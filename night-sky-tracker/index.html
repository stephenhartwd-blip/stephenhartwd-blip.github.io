Common questions

Why didn't I get an alert?

We only alert when there's something worth seeing and your sky is forecast to be clear and dark enough. Check that notifications are on (iOS Settings → Night Sky Tracker → Notifications), that location is allowed, and your "Alert me" settings in the app. Focus modes can also hold notifications back.

How is the meteor count worked out?

We start from each shower's peak rate under perfect skies (the International Meteor Organization's figures), then allow for how high the radiant is above you, twilight, moonlight and the light pollution at your spot. It's an estimate: real rates vary from year to year and minute to minute.

How accurate is the light-pollution map?

It's an estimate made from NASA's public-domain satellite images of Earth at night, spread out to model sky glow, and shown as a Bortle class from 1 (truly dark) to 9 (inner city). It's a good guide for finding darker skies, not a precise measurement.

The compass points the wrong way

Move away from cars and metal, then wave your phone in a figure-8 to recalibrate it. Check that Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → System Services → Compass Calibration is on.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Night Sky Tracker. Cancelling stops future renewals, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought it but it's asking me to pay again

In the app, open the paywall or Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I report or remove a photo?

Tap the photo, then Report or Block. To delete your own, tap it and choose Delete. Photos disappear automatically after 30 days.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Community photos → Delete account & my photos. This removes everything you've posted.
