Night Sky Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Night Sky Tracker tells you when and where to look up: meteor showers, the ISS, planets and dark, clear nights. We collect as little as possible to do that, we never sell your data, and the app contains no ads or third-party tracking.

What we collect, and why

  • Approximate location. Used on your device to work out conditions, light pollution and event times. If you turn on alerts, we store your location rounded to about 10 km (and any saved spots, also rounded) with its estimated light-pollution class, so our server can tell when something is worth seeing there.
  • Push notification token. Lets us send you night sky alerts. Stored only under a one-way hash. Turning alerts off removes it.
  • Alert settings. Your sensitivity, quiet hours and time zone, so alerts arrive when you want them.
  • Photos you choose to post. If you sign in with Apple and post a photo, we store the photo, your caption, your display name and the location rounded to about 5 km. Photos are re-encoded on your phone, which removes their original metadata (including exact GPS). Photos are public and are automatically deleted after 30 days.
  • Sign in with Apple. We receive an anonymous Apple user identifier and, the first time only, the name you choose to share. We never see your password, and you can use Apple's "Hide My Email".
  • Purchases. Handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no ad networks, no analytics SDKs, no selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No background location tracking. Location is only read while you use the app.

Services we use

  • CelesTrak for the Space Station's orbit (no personal data is sent), and MET Norway (weather data from MET Norway, CC BY 4.0) and the US National Weather Service for cloud forecasts. Requests contain only rounded coordinates.
  • Apple for push notifications, Sign in with Apple, weather (outside the US), maps and purchases.
  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud to store alert settings and photos, and to check posted photos for inappropriate content.

Community rules and moderation

Posted photos must be real photos of the night sky with no objectionable content. Anyone can report a photo or block a person. Reported content is hidden and reviewed, and accounts that break the rules are removed.

Your choices

  • Delete your account and photos: Settings → Community photos → Delete account. This removes everything you posted.
  • Stop alerts: turn off notifications for Night Sky Tracker in iOS Settings, or change "Alert me" in the app.
  • Location: you can deny or change location access in iOS Settings at any time.

EU, EEA and UK users (GDPR / UK GDPR)

  • Who is responsible: Stephen Hart, publisher of Night Sky Tracker, is the data controller. Contact: stephenhartwd@gmail.com.
  • Legal bases: providing the service you asked for (contract), which covers forecasts, alerts, your account and your photos. Your consent covers location and notifications, which you can withdraw at any time in iOS Settings. Our legitimate interests cover keeping the service safe and moderating content.
  • How long we keep data: photos for 30 days. Alert settings until you turn alerts off or delete the app's registration. Account data until you delete your account. Moderation reports for up to 12 months.
  • International transfers: data is processed in the United States by Google (Firebase) and Apple, under their standard data protection terms, including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where applicable.
  • Your rights: you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent. Email us and we'll reply within one month. You can delete your account yourself in the app at any time.
  • Complaints: you can complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).
  • We don't sell personal data, use it for advertising, or make automated decisions with legal effects about you.

Children

Night Sky Tracker is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.
