Aurora Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Aurora Tracker tells you when the northern lights are visible from where you are. We collect as little as possible to do that, we never sell your data, and the app contains no ads or third-party tracking.

What we collect, and why

  • Approximate location. Used on your device to calculate visibility. If you turn on alerts, we store your location rounded to about 10 km (and any saved spots, also rounded) so our server can tell when the aurora is visible there.
  • Push notification token. Lets us send you aurora alerts. Stored only under a one-way hash. Turning alerts off removes it.
  • Alert settings. Your sensitivity, quiet hours and time zone, so alerts arrive when you want them.
  • Sightings you choose to post. If you sign in with Apple and post a sighting, we store the photo, your caption, your display name and the location rounded to about 5 km. Photos are re-encoded on your phone, which removes their original metadata (including exact GPS). Sightings are public and are automatically deleted after 30 days.
  • Sign in with Apple. We receive an anonymous Apple user identifier and, the first time only, the name you choose to share. We never see your password, and you can use Apple's "Hide My Email".
  • Purchases. Handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no ad networks, no analytics SDKs, no selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No background location tracking. Location is only read while you use the app.

Services we use

  • NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center and the US National Weather Service for aurora and cloud data. Requests contain only rounded coordinates.
  • Apple for push notifications, Sign in with Apple, weather (outside the US), maps and purchases.
  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud to store alert settings and sightings, and to check posted photos for inappropriate content.

Community rules and moderation

Posted sightings must be real photos of the sky with no objectionable content. Anyone can report a sighting or block a person. Reported content is hidden and reviewed, and accounts that break the rules are removed.

Your choices

  • Delete your account and sightings: Settings → Community sightings → Delete account. This removes everything you posted.
  • Stop alerts: turn off notifications for Aurora Tracker in iOS Settings, or change "Alert me" in the app.
  • Location: you can deny or change location access in iOS Settings at any time.

Children

Aurora Tracker is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.
