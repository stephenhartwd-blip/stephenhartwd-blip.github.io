Common questions

Why didn't I get an alert?

We only alert when the aurora is likely and your sky is dark and mostly clear. Check that notifications are on (iOS Settings → Aurora Tracker → Notifications), that location is allowed, and your "Alert me" setting in the app. Focus modes can also hold notifications back.

The app says "Not tonight" but people are seeing it

Aurora forecasts are guidance, not guarantees. Check the "Seen near you" card for real photos from people nearby, and try your phone camera's Night mode pointed toward the pole (north, or south in the southern hemisphere).

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Aurora Tracker. Cancelling stops future renewals, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought it but it's asking me to pay again

In the app, open the paywall or Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I report or remove a sighting?

Tap the sighting, then Report or Block. To delete your own, tap it and choose Delete my sighting. Sightings disappear automatically after 30 days.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Community sightings → Delete account & my sightings. This removes everything you've posted.
